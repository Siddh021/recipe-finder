Frequently Asked Questions

How do I search for specific recipes?

Use the search bar at the top of the Recipes page to type in recipe names or ingredients. You can also use the category dropdown to filter by Main Course, Dessert, Quick Meals, and more.

What do the icons mean on recipe cards?

The blue badge shows the recipe category (Main Course, Dessert, etc.). The green box displays cooking time. Below each recipe, you'll see icons for servings and a "View Recipe" button to see full details.

How do I view the full recipe details?

Click the "View Recipe" button on any recipe card to see the complete recipe. You'll find detailed information including cook time, servings, category, difficulty level, ingredients list, and step-by-step instructions.

What information is shown on the recipe detail page?

The recipe detail page shows: recipe name, description, cooking time, servings, category, difficulty level, a large image, ingredients list with quantities, and step-by-step instructions. There's also a "Back to Recipes" button for easy navigation.

How are recipes categorized?

Recipes are categorized as Main Course, Dessert, Quick Meals, Healthy Options, and more. Each recipe card displays its category in a blue badge, and you can filter recipes by category using the dropdown menu on the Recipes page.

Can I adjust serving sizes for recipes?

Yes! On each recipe detail page, you'll see the number of servings (like "8 slices" for desserts). While we don't have automatic scaling yet, you can manually adjust ingredient quantities based on the original serving size.
